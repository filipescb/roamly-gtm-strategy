# Roamly Groups · Competitive Battlecard & Strategic Bet, Module 2 (Exercise 2)

## Responses
- **Who is Roamly Groups actually for, based on everything you now know?:** Not "celebration trip organizers" broadly — the organizer coordinating a multi-day, multi-activity trip for a larger group (6+ people), where coordination load is high enough that DIY tools start failing. Occasion was never the real qualifier; complexity is.
- **What is the one thing that makes it worth choosing over the competitor?:** One connected flow — booking, split payment, and itinerary in a single system — versus a stitched-together stack of disconnected free tools that never sync with each other.
- **What is the biggest threat to that position, based on your battlecard?:** Below the complexity threshold, the advantage disappears. For a simple 3-4 person trip, DIY wins on pure effort every time — the threat isn't a better competitor, it's that most trips may never generate enough pain to justify switching.
- **Of all the signals you’re seeing, which one changes how you think about the market? Hint: looking at how your primary competitor wins, what does that tell you about what Roamly Groups needs to do differently to take that buyer?:** Airbnb's split payments have existed since 2018, free, inside an app everyone already trusts. Split payments as a mechanic is commoditized — the real gap is connecting the payment to the itinerary and the activity. Nobody owns that layer. The fight isn't "build a better payment feature," it's "own the coordination layer for experience-based trips."
- **Complete the sentence: “I now believe that Roamly Groups wins when because”:** "I now believe that Roamly Groups wins when the trip is complex enough that no single free tool can hold it together — because that's when the DIY stack's core weakness (nothing talks to anything else) turns from annoyance into failure."
- **“I am betting that Roamly Groups wins by”, your strategic commitment, not what you’re building but how you’ll win with it.:** "I am betting that Roamly Groups wins by being the system of record for complex group trips — not by being the easiest way to split a bill."
- **“To make this bet, I am deprioritizing”, every strategic bet means giving something up. Naming the sacrifice is the proof a real decision was made.:** "To make this bet, I am deprioritizing simple, low-complexity celebration trips as a v1 target — even though they fall inside my original segment — because that's exactly where the free DIY stack wins on effort alone."
