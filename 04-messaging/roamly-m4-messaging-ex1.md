# Roamly Groups · Messaging Pillars & AI-Generated Asset, Module 4 (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **FROM (Life before):** Planning a trip with friends means one person quietly becomes the group's unpaid project manager. Whoever cares most, or speaks up first, ends up fronting money, tracking who owes what across a dozen Venmo requests, and repeating the same plan five times because half the group only half-read the group chat. The bigger and more meaningful the trip — a milestone birthday, a reunion, someone's last hurrah before a wedding — the heavier that invisible labor gets, and the more likely it is to quietly sour the thing that was supposed to bring everyone closer.
- **TO (Life after):** Coordinating a group trip stops depending on one person's patience and memory. Money, plans, and commitment move through the group on their own, so no single friend has to carry the weight of making sure everyone else shows up, pays up, and knows what's happening. Planning something together stops being a chore one person absorbs on everyone else's behalf, and goes back to being what it was supposed to be — a shared thing, done together, before the trip has even started.
- **Total number of audiences that need their own message:** Just 1, the trip organizer
- **Justification, why does each one need its own pillar?:** Just the trip organizer - the friend running a multi-day, multi-activity celebration trip for 6+ people.

I'm not adding the participant/guest as a second audience, they're not a buyer or decision-maker, they just tap a payment link the organizer already sent. No messaging is ever aimed at them directly.

I'm also not splitting simple vs. complex trip organizers into two audiences. That's a targeting call, not a messaging one, the M2 bet already deprioritized simple trips, so there's nothing to message there.

And I'm leaving out corporate offsite organizers, a real alternative segment from M1, but explicitly a Phase 2 motion, not part of this launch.

One sharp audience beats three diluted ones for a v1 launch built on narrowing, not broadening.
- **Audience 1:** Trip Organizer
- **🎯 Value Prop (The promise):** You get to go back to being a friend on the trip instead of the one running it - no more carrying the coordination on your patience and memory alone.
- **🏆 Capabilities (The proof points):** - One shared link the whole group books through
- Automatic split payments, no fronting costs or chasing reimbursement
- Shared itinerary everyone can see, no re-explaining the plan
- Guests pay and view the plan without creating an account
- Built on Roamly's vetted local experts, not generic tours
- **📊 Evidence (The receipts):** - 3x industry-average repeat booking rate
- Support tickets show users already DIY-ing this pain today
- 12,000+ vetted experts across 40 cities, four continents
- Majority of travelers report frequent group trips (per Airbnb's own research)
