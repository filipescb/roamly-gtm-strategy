# Roamly Groups — Positioning Framework (First Draft)

Positioning the group booking, split-payment, and shared-itinerary experience for the celebration-trip organizer

## 01 · FOR — Who specifically is this for?

The organizer in a friend group planning a multi-day, multi-activity celebration trip for 6+ people — a bachelorette weekend, a reunion, a milestone birthday.

## 02 · WHO WANT/NEED — What problem are they solving, in their words?

"I'm sick of chasing everyone for money, re-explaining the plan, and never knowing who's actually confirmed."

## 03 · WE OFFER — What is Roamly Groups, in plain terms?

A way to plan a group trip on Roamly where everyone books, pays their own share, and sees the same shared itinerary — all in one place.

## 04 · BECAUSE OF — How does it meaningfully improve their situation?

The organizer stops being the group's unpaid project manager, and gets their time back to actually look forward to the trip.

## 05 · DESPITE — The shortcoming we don't get to skip

Every guest still has to adopt a new platform for something — splitting a bill, checking a plan — that Airbnb already offers for free, in an app they trust.

## 06 · BETTER THAN — Why are we the better choice, defensibly?

Airbnb solved lodging payments, not activities. Roamly Groups connects booking, payment, and itinerary in one flow — backed by 12,000+ vetted local experts and a 3x repeat-booking rate Airbnb's Experiences can't match.

---

## Working Statement

For the organizer coordinating a multi-day trip for 6+ friends who wants to stop chasing payments and juggling bookings, Roamly Groups is a group booking, payment, and itinerary tool — because it turns them back into a guest on their own trip, despite asking friends to adopt a platform Airbnb lets them avoid for free, since no competitor connects the booking, payment, and plan the way Roamly Groups does.
