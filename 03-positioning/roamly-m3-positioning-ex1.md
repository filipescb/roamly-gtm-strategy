# Roamly Groups · Positioning Framework & Statement, Module 3 (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **FOR:** The organizer in a friend group planning a multi-day, multi-activity celebration trip for 6 or more people — a bachelorette weekend, a reunion, a milestone birthday — who has already taken on the job of pulling the trip together for everyone else.
- **WHO WANT / NEED:** "I'm sick of being the one chasing everyone for money, re-explaining the plan five times, and never actually knowing who's confirmed until the week before we leave."
- **WE OFFER:** A way to plan a group trip on Roamly where everyone books, pays their own share, and sees the same shared itinerary — all in one place, instead of juggling separate bookings, group chats, and payment apps.
- **BECAUSE OF:** The organizer stops being the group's unpaid project manager. They get their time and headspace back to actually look forward to the trip, instead of spending weeks doing unpaid logistics work for other people's vacation.
- **DESPITE:** Every person in the group still has to engage with a platform they may have never used before, for a job (splitting a bill, checking an itinerary) that Airbnb already lets them do for free, inside an app they already have open and trust. We are asking for adoption effort that the free alternative doesn't require.
- **BETTER THAN:** Airbnb solved splitting a lodging bill; it never built a connected system for the activities layer of a trip. Roamly Groups is purpose-built so the booking, the payment, and the itinerary are one synced flow — not three disconnected tools bolted together — and it's backed by 12,000+ vetted local experts and a 3x industry-average repeat booking rate, a trust signal Airbnb's twice-relaunched Experiences program can't currently match.
