# Roamly Groups · Pricing & Packaging Recommendation, Module 5 (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **Pricing goal (Where to win first):** Defended position vs. status quo/Airbnb — not new market, not revenue maximization.
- **Why (Justification):** M2 bet wins on positioning, not revenue. Both real competitors are free; charging now hands them an easy objection. Win the position first, let revenue follow.
- **Pricing model (The model):** Transaction-based, per booking — no subscription (infrequent use), no seats (variable group size). Matches PLG motion: self-serve, no plan to pick.
- **Who pays (Buyer vs. user):** Traveler pays for their own booking, as today (existing commission). Organizer pays nothing extra — coordination is free.
- **Packaging (The structure):** One audience (organizer) = one structure, no tiers. Tiering would force a decision the DIY stack doesn't require.
- **Tiers (What each is for):** None. Single included capability — same for every organizer. (Corporate offsites, if pursued later, gets its own separate structure, not a tier.)
- **Which research method (Van Westendorp, Max-Diff, Conjoint) would you commission, and what specific assumption are you trying to validate?:** No Van Westendorp/Max-Diff/Conjoint — wrong tools since we're not pricing a fee. Live usage pilot instead: zero-fee launch to repeat bookers, testing whether that's enough to convert people off Airbnb/DIY.
- **What is the one thing in your recommendation that would change everything if you got it wrong?:** That group trips actually generate more revenue per trip than solo bookings. If not, there's no monetization mechanism at all.
